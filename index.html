PC troubleshooting, with your approval

When a task needs a system command, YUNA explains a possible fix and shows you exactly what she plans to run. Use it to inspect logs, check a stuck port, or handle a repetitive task. Nothing runs until you approve it.

Describe the issueFor example: “Why is port 3000 stuck?”
Review the suggestionYUNA shows the command and what it is meant to check.
Choose whether to run itYou approve the command, or decline it. Your choice is explicit.
Understand the resultYUNA reads the output and explains what it means in plain language.
Always blocked: deleting system folders, wiping drives, shutdown or reboot, registry deletion, changing accounts and passwords, turning off the firewall or Defender.

Questions

Does YUNA need internet?
Local chat with an already-downloaded Ollama model can work offline. Internet is needed for web search, downloads, and cloud AI providers. When you use a cloud provider, your prompts are sent to that provider. YUNA does not add its own telemetry.
Can I use my own OpenAI or Claude key?
Yes. YUNA supports OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Google Gemini, Groq and more. Enter your key in settings.
Does it run without a GPU?
Yes. An i5 with 8 GB RAM is enough. A GPU only makes larger models faster.
Can I change her personality?
Completely. Edit the prompt in settings, or create separate characters.
What is the refund policy?
If YUNA doesn’t work on your machine and we can’t fix it, you may request a refund within 7 days, minus any transfer fees or taxes.
Mac or Linux?
Windows 10 and 11 only for now. Mac and Linux are planned, free for existing licence holders.
How do I move my licence to a new PC?
Contact us on Discord or email with your key and proof of purchase. We deactivate the old device and reactivate the new one, free of charge.
