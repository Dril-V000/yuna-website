Questions

Does YUNA need internet?
Only on first launch to download the AI model, and for web search or cloud AI providers if you use them. Local chat with Ollama works offline, with no telemetry.
Can I use my own OpenAI or Claude key?
Yes. YUNA supports OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Google Gemini, Groq and more. Enter your key in settings.
Does it run without a GPU?
Yes. An i5 with 8 GB RAM is enough. A GPU only makes larger models faster.
Can I change her personality?
Completely. Edit the prompt in settings, or create separate characters.
What is the refund policy?
If YUNA doesn’t work on your machine and we can’t fix it, you may request a refund within 7 days, minus any transfer fees or taxes.
Mac or Linux?
Windows 10 and 11 only for now. Mac and Linux are planned, free for existing licence holders.
How do I move my licence to a new PC?
Contact us on Discord or email with your key and proof of purchase. We deactivate the old device and reactivate the new one, free of charge.
