Choose the brain behind YUNA.

YUNA is the companion interface: voice conversations, a reactive Live2D character and desktop tools. You choose which AI provider powers the conversation — locally on your PC or through a cloud API.

Run AI locally with Ollama

Connect a compatible model running on your own computer. Once the model is downloaded, local conversations can work without an internet connection.

🖥️

Your computer runs the model

YUNA connects to Ollama on your PC. Model performance depends on your processor, memory, graphics hardware and the model you select.

🔒

Keep local prompts on-device

When you use a local Ollama model, your conversation is processed locally rather than sent to a cloud AI provider.

New to local models? Start with the system requirements and choose a model that fits your available RAM and hardware. Larger models are not automatically better if your PC cannot run them comfortably.

Connect a cloud provider

Prefer a hosted model? Add your own API key in YUNA's settings and use a provider and model available to your account.
